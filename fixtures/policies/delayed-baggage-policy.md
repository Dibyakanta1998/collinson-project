# Delayed Baggage Policy — Demonstration Fixture

Policy version: CL-DEMO-2026-01

This fictional policy exists only for the ClaimLens AI technical exercise.

## 4.2 Delayed checked baggage

We may reimburse reasonable essential purchases when checked baggage is delayed
for more than six consecutive hours after the traveller reaches their scheduled
destination.

## 4.3 Required evidence

The claimant must provide:

1. the airline property irregularity or baggage report;
2. the travel itinerary showing the scheduled destination; and
3. itemised receipts for any essential purchases being claimed.

Missing receipts require manual review and do not by themselves establish that
the claim is ineligible.

## 4.4 Exclusions

Cover does not apply when the delay resulted from baggage being detained by
customs or another public authority, or when the baggage was not checked with
the operating carrier.

## 4.5 Decision boundary

All automated findings are provisional. A claims handler must approve the
result before it is communicated as a coverage decision.
